# Asset provenance

All geometry, 128px deterministic surface textures, synthesized sound effects,
HUD, avatars and weapon silhouettes are authored in this repository. No extracted
game meshes, textures, audio, logos or fonts are included. System fonts only.
Procedural source: `shared/map.ts`, `client/scene.ts`, `client/audio.ts`.

Project-authored code/assets are MIT licensed. Dependency licenses are shipped
in node_modules in the image and captured by package-lock.json: Three.js MIT,
Rapier Apache-2.0, Fastify MIT, ws MIT, Vite MIT, TypeScript Apache-2.0.

Highrise, Intervention, MW2 and Call of Duty identify the inspiration; this
independent community project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Activision.
Map layout and nostalgic feel are pending human reference/playtest review.
