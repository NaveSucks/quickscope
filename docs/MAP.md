# Map draft and route checklist

Coordinates are metres, Y up. Map origin is the raised central helipad; offices
face each other on Z=-33 and Z=33. Construction deck is Y=0, connector Y=-4,
office roofs Y=5.3. Approximate footprint is 44 x 82 metres. These dimensions are
initial authored estimates, not measurements of the original game.

Reference leads (not imported assets):
- Original MW2 crane traversal: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BJYhZ620SCY

Jump initial velocity 8m/s, gravity 22m/s², sprint 8.4m/s gives approximately
1.45m apex and 6.1m same-height horizontal range. Ladders and authored mantle
points provide necessary greater elevation changes. No invisible arena walls;
falls below Y=-15 kill without changing kill score.
